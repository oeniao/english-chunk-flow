Many people start the day with a long list of tasks.
Although every task seems important, a clear plan can help us decide what to do first.
Before the first meeting begins, we can choose one task that needs our full attention.
If a new request arrives, we do not have to change the whole plan immediately.
A short break is useful because it gives us time to think.
The goal is to finish the work that matters most, rather than stay busy all day.
